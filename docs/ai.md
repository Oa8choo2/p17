# AI

AI Resist list\
<https://airesistlist.org/#top> Outlines practical ways in which you can ‘resist’
AI, and explains why.
It also highlights inspiring examples of best practice showing how AI can be
used ethically in the future.

Greenpeace article on the impact of AI on democracy and the environment
<https://www.greenpeace.org/international/story/82486/ai-energy-environment-democracy/>\
An overview of a number of clear issues surrounding the use of AI in various
areas. Clear links to the organisation’s own vision and mission, e.g. the
construction of data centres = negative local consequences, profits are
‘exported’ to the super-rich, or, for example, the link between war and AI

Quit GPT <https://quitgpt.org/#why> Call for a boycott of ChatGPT and other AI
technology from OpenAI. Focuses mainly on the arguments against this specific
company (largest sponsor of Trump and ICE campaigns…), but some arguments also
apply to other GenAI companies.

Library of Babel Group initiative
<https://www.law.georgetown.edu/privacy-technology-center/education/the-library-of-babel-group/>
An initiative by the Georgetown Law Privacy & Technology Centre, linked to an
open call for anyone involved in education to refrain from using GenAI. It also
provides clear reasoning for this, offering a different perspective: why we do
not want GenAI in education.

Fairly Trained certification <https://www.fairlytrained.org/> 
An organisation
that researches AI technologies and certifies
